## What is CareerForge Analytics?

CareerForge Analytics is an immersive career acceleration programme combining real-world job simulations, expert mentorship, live projects, performance reviews, and career coaching to bridge the gap between learning and employment.

## What tracks are available?

CareerForge offers Training and Internship application types across Data, Product, Software, and Design tracks. Internship applicants additionally submit a CV, GitHub or portfolio URL, and LinkedIn URL.

## How long does the programme run?

The combined curriculum runs 12 weeks, covering Excel, SQL, Power BI, and Python for Data Analytics, delivered on a Monday, Wednesday, and Friday cadence.

## How do I apply?

Visit the Apply page, select Training or Internship, complete all required application fields, and proceed to payment through Paystack.

## How do I contact CareerForge?

Use the Contact page for questions that are not answered here or to request help from the CareerForge team.
